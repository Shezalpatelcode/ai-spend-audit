# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target Audience

The platform targets:
- startups
- engineering teams
- AI-first companies
- operations managers
- SaaS organizations managing multiple AI subscriptions

---

# Problem Being Solved

Many organizations adopt AI tools rapidly without visibility into:
- overlapping subscriptions
- redundant tooling
- unnecessary enterprise upgrades
- inefficient team allocation

This creates avoidable operational spending.

---

# Product Positioning

The platform is positioned as:
> a lightweight AI spend optimization and visibility tool.

Primary value proposition:
- reduce AI tooling waste
- simplify AI stack decisions
- improve cost efficiency

---
