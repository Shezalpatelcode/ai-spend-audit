# User Interview Notes

## Goal

The purpose of these interviews was to better understand how teams currently manage AI tooling and subscription costs.

---

# Key Observations

## Lack of Visibility

Users often lacked centralized visibility into:
- AI subscriptions
- overlapping tooling
- total operational spend

---

## Rapid Tool Adoption

Teams frequently adopted new AI tools quickly without:
- procurement review
- cost tracking
- optimization analysis

---

## Interest in Consolidation

Many users expressed interest in:
- reducing overlapping subscriptions
- simplifying workflows
- lowering operational costs

---

# Common Pain Points

- unclear pricing structures
- duplicated tooling
- unmanaged seat growth
- inconsistent usage patterns
- lack of reporting

---

# Product Insights

The interviews reinforced the value of:
- quick audit workflows
- simple reporting
- shareable summaries
- lightweight onboarding

---

# Future Research Areas

Potential future interview topics:
- enterprise procurement workflows
- AI usage tracking
- budgeting processes
- vendor comparison needs
